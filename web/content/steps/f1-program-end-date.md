#### What it is

Your I-20 shows a program end date.
Your OPT window and your grace period both count from it.

#### When

If you need more time to finish, ask your DSO for a program extension before that date.

#### Forms and fees

No USCIS form or fee.
Your DSO extends the program in SEVIS and gives you an updated I-20.

#### What to watch for

- The extension must be requested before the end date passes.
- Changing schools or degree levels follows SEVIS transfer rules. Talk to your DSO first.
