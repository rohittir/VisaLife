#### What it is

A child must be under 21 to get a green card as your derivative.
The Child Status Protection Act (CSPA) can lower a child's age for this purpose.

#### When

It matters once a visa becomes available, and most for children close to 21.

#### Forms and fees

No separate form. The child files an I-485 like other family members.

#### What to watch for

- CSPA age is the child's age when a visa becomes available, minus the time the I-140 was pending.
- A visa becomes available on the later of the I-140 approval date and the first day of the month the Final Action Dates chart shows the priority date as current.
- The child must seek to acquire the green card within one year of that date, for example by filing the I-485.
- A child who will still age out may need a status of their own, such as F-1.
