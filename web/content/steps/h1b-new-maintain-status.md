#### What it is

H-1B lets you work only for the employer that filed your petition, in the job and place it describes.
Work that is not authorized is a failure to maintain status.
That includes freelance or side work for anyone else.

#### When

This applies every day you are in H-1B status.
Check your I-94 each time you enter the US.

#### Forms and fees

Report a new home address to USCIS within 10 days of moving. Your online account is the preferred way. Form AR-11 on paper also counts.
There is no fee.

#### What to watch for

- A second job needs its own H-1B petition from that employer.
- Changing employers goes through portability. See the Changing employer group.
- A material change in your job or place of work needs an amended petition before you make it. See the amendment step.
- If your job ends, your 60-day grace period is in the Laid off or benched group.
- Keep your I-797, I-94 and pay stubs together. You may need them for extensions and for a green card.
