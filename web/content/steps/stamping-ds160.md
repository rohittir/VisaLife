#### What it is

The DS-160 is the online visa application. You book your appointment in India with its confirmation number.
India requires a properly completed DS-160: the right name, sex, visa class, date of birth and passport number for the person who will attend.
A blank or placeholder DS-160 loses you the appointment.
Fill it in yourself, even if an agent helps you with the rest.

#### When

Before you start looking for slots, so you can book the moment one opens.
- H-1B: you can apply up to 90 days before the start date on your I-797. You cannot enter more than 10 days before it unless you already work for that employer. The petition must be approved first.
- H-4: apply with the H-1B holder. India recommends that the family interview together.
- F-1, new program: a visa can be issued up to 365 days before the start date. You cannot enter more than 30 days before it.

#### Forms and fees

- One DS-160 and one visa fee for each person, children included.
- A submitted DS-160 can be used to schedule for one year.
- Write down the application ID. You need it to return to the form.
- An unfinished form is kept for 30 days. Save it to your computer to keep it longer.
- You can reuse answers from an earlier DS-160 to fill a new one. Check every field, because old answers may be out of date.
- Print the confirmation page. You bring it to every appointment.

#### What to watch for

- List your social media accounts and set them to public. India's embassy asks this of H-1B, H-4 and F-1 applicants.
- Use the passport you will carry to the appointment.
- Choose the visa class carefully. H-4 is the dependent class for H-1B spouses and children.
- If something changes after you submit, see the step on changing your DS-160.
