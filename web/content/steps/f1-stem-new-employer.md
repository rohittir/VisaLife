#### What it is

You can change employers during STEM OPT.
The new employer must meet the same STEM OPT rules, including E-Verify.

#### When

- The new employer must be enrolled in E-Verify before you start paid work.
- Send a new I-983 to your DSO within 10 days of starting.
- Send the final evaluation for the old job within 10 days of leaving it.

#### Forms and fees

A new Form I-983. No USCIS filing or fee.

#### What to watch for

- If the gap between jobs is longer than 10 days, report the job loss first, then send the new I-983.
- Days between jobs count toward your 150 unemployment days.
