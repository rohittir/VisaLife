#### What it is

Your H-4 status does not extend by itself when your spouse's H-1B is extended.
You extend it with Form I-539.
Your children can be added to the same application with Form I-539A.

#### When

File before your current I-94 expires.
Most families file it together with the employer's H-1B extension petition (Form I-129).
You can also file it on its own, as long as your I-94 has not expired.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-539: $470 on paper. The online fee is $420 where online filing is available for your case.
- Premium processing is not available for an H-4 I-539 on its own. When it is filed with the H-1B petition, USCIS decides it alongside that petition.
- USCIS is accepting the 08/28/24 edition of Form I-539 right now. Check the form page for the edition to use.

#### What to watch for

- Include every family member who needs more time.
- If you hold an H-4 EAD, file its renewal at the same time. USCIS decides the EAD only after the I-539.
