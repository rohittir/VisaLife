#### What it is

USCIS sends an approval notice first and the card a little later.
Your permanent residence is recorded from the approval date, the "resident since" date on the card.
Employment-based green cards are normally valid for 10 years. Only marriage-based and investor cards are conditional.

#### When

If the card has not arrived 30 days after the notice or after the status shows it was mailed, file an e-Request with USCIS.
A temporary I-551 stamp in your passport is proof of your status. If the stamp has no end date, it is valid for one year.

#### Forms and fees

No form is needed.
Take your green card or I-551 stamp when you apply for a Social Security card without work restrictions.

#### What to watch for

- You no longer need H-1B status. Set your stage to green card approved in Me.
- Report every change of address within 10 days. Green card holders must also carry the card.
- Men aged 18 through 25 must register with the Selective Service, including immigrants.
- Your spouse and children each get their own approval if they applied with you.
- VisaLife does not cover life as a permanent resident yet.
