#### What it is

Under INA 204(j), a pending I-485 can survive a job change.
You must meet all of these:

- your I-485 has been pending 180 days or more,
- your I-140 is approved, or is pending and later approved, and
- the new job is in the same or a similar occupational classification, and is a full-time, permanent offer.

#### When

File Supplement J only after the 180 days and after you have the new job offer.
USCIS rejects a Supplement J that is filed too early.

#### Forms and fees

You file Form I-485 Supplement J. Your new employer completes and signs its part.
Include the new employer's offer letter.

#### What to watch for

- "Same or similar" is a whole-picture test. USCIS compares duties, job titles, skills, education, wage and the occupation codes.
- Supplement J does not decide whether you may work for the new employer. You still need a new H-1B petition or an EAD.
- If your I-485 has been pending under 180 days, the approved I-140 does not carry the case to a new employer.
- EB-1A and NIW applicants do not need Supplement J.
- Moving jobs is a strategic decision. Talk to an immigration attorney first.
