#### What it is

Most non-citizens must get a certificate of compliance from the IRS before they leave the US. People call it a sailing permit or a departure permit.
It shows the IRS that your US tax is paid or covered.

#### When

Apply at least 2 weeks before you leave and no more than 30 days before.
Appointments can be limited, so book early.

#### Forms and fees

Who needs one:

- H-1B holders. H-1B is not on the IRS list of people who are exempt.
- H-4 holders are exempt if they entered on H-4 and their only US income is study or training allowances, employment authorized under immigration law, or bank interest. Other income, such as dividends or capital gains, takes you outside the exemption.
- F-1 holders are exempt on the same income test.

The forms:

- Form 2063 is for people with no taxable US income for the year through departure (and none the year before, if that return is not yet due), and for resident aliens whose leaving will not make tax harder to collect.
- Form 1040-C is for everyone else. It reports your income through departure, and you pay the tax with it.

You apply at an IRS Taxpayer Assistance Center, by appointment. The number is 844-545-5640.
Bring your passport and visa, your last two years of returns, a statement from your employer of wages and withholding from January 1 to your departure, and proof of your departure date such as a ticket.

#### What to watch for

- OPT is not named on the IRS page. OPT work is employment authorized under immigration law, so it falls under that wording. Ask your DSO or a CPA if you are unsure.
- An F-1 student who changed to H-1B is no longer exempt.
- The IRS decides which form you get. A resident alien who is leaving for good can be asked for Form 1040-C.
- Form 1040-C is not your annual return. You still file Form 1040-NR after the year ends. The tax you paid counts as a credit.
- The regulation says an officer can examine you at the point of departure if you have neither a certificate nor proof of exemption.
- If you are married to someone who is also leaving, both of you go to the appointment.
- Talk to a CPA before you book if you are on H-1B, sold assets this year or have a case pending.
