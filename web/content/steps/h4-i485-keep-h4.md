#### What it is

Travel abroad does not abandon your I-485 if all of these are true:

- your spouse is maintaining H-1B status,
- you are in lawful H-4 status and still eligible for it, and
- you have a valid H-4 visa stamp, if you need one.

#### When

For as long as your I-485 is pending and you keep H-4 status.

#### Forms and fees

To keep H-4 status, you still extend it with Form I-539 when your I-94 runs out.
The fee is $470 on paper.

#### What to watch for

- If your H-4 status lapses, you need advance parole to travel without abandoning the I-485.
- Each trip needs a valid visa stamp. Check its date before you book.
