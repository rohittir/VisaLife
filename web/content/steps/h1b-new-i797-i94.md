#### What it is

- Your I-797 is a USCIS approval notice. It shows the dates your H-1B petition is valid.
- Your I-94 is a CBP record created when you enter the US. Its "Admit Until" date is the date your status ends.

CBP may admit you for the petition's validity period plus up to 10 days before it starts and 10 days after it ends.
You may work only during the validity period.

#### When

Check your I-94 every time you enter the US.
Download it free from the CBP I-94 website.

#### Forms and fees

No form is needed to read or download your I-94.
If CBP got your class, personal details or period of admission wrong, go to a CBP deferred inspection office or a port of entry. Do not file Form I-102 for a record CBP issued.

#### What to watch for

- If your I-94 date is earlier than your I-797 end date, treat the I-94 date as your deadline until it is corrected.
- One petition lasts up to three years and cannot run past the validity of its LCA.
- Your visa stamp is a third document. Its expiry date is not your status deadline.
- Keep a copy of every I-797 and I-94 you have ever received.
