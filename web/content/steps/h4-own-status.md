#### What it is

Your H-4 status and EAD depend on your spouse.
Some H-4 spouses move to a status of their own.
The two common routes are H-1B through your own employer and F-1 as a student.

#### When

Your H-4 status must still be valid when the new application is filed.

#### Forms and fees

- H-1B: your employer registers you in the H-1B lottery ($215) and files Form I-129 if you are selected.
- F-1: you need a Form I-20 from a school, then Form I-539 to change status ($470 on paper, $420 online).
- Premium processing for a change to F-1 costs $2,075.

#### What to watch for

- The H-1B lottery is now weighted by the wage level of the job offer. Level IV counts four times, Level I counts once.
- Do not change what you do until USCIS approves the change of status.
- The $100,000 H-1B payment does not apply when USCIS grants a change of status inside the US. A court vacated the payment guidance in June 2026, so check the USCIS H-1B page before filing.
