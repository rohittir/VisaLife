#### What it is

Federal law does not set a deadline for your final paycheck. Some states require it right away.
Federal wage law also does not require pay for vacation, sick leave or holidays.
Whether unused PTO is paid out depends on your state and your employer's policy.

#### When

Ask HR before your last day.
Get three answers in writing: when your final pay arrives, whether your PTO is paid out, and the date your health cover ends.

#### Forms and fees

Your Form W-2 is due to you by the end of January. For pay in 2026 the date is Feb. 1, 2027.
If your job ends during the year, your employer may send it earlier.
If you ask for it, they must give it to you within 30 days of your request or of your final pay, whichever is later.

#### What to watch for

- Your employer meets the W-2 rule by mailing it to the address on file. Give HR a mailing address that will still work after you move.
- If your W-2 has not come by the end of February, ask your employer first. Then call the IRS at 800-829-1040. From abroad, the IRS lists +1 267-941-1000 for international taxpayers.
- A mail forward for a move outside the US must be filed in person at a Post Office before you leave. USPS does not forward mail from banks and government agencies, so tell each sender yourself.
- If pay is missing or wrong, contact your state labor department. The list of state offices is in the sources. An employment lawyer can help if that does not work.
