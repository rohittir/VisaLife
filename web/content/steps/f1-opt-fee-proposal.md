#### What it is

On Oct. 8, 2026, DHS proposed new fees for OPT.
The proposal would charge $70,000 per student for initial OPT and $30,000 for any later OPT.
Your school would pay it before the DSO recommends you. Schools could pass the cost on to students or employers.

#### When

This is only a proposal. Public comments are due by Nov. 9, 2026.
If it becomes final, it would apply to DSO recommendations dated on or after the effective date.
DHS proposes that date as 60 days after the final rule is published.

#### Forms and fees

Nothing changes today.
The OPT filing fee is still $470 online or $520 on paper.

#### What to watch for

- Ask your school how it would handle the fee if the rule becomes final.
- Under the proposal, a recommendation dated before the effective date is not charged.
