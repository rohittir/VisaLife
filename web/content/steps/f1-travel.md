#### What it is

To return from a trip, you need a valid passport, a valid F-1 visa stamp, and an I-20 with a recent DSO travel signature.

#### When

- While you study, a DSO travel signature is valid for one year.
- On post-completion OPT, the signature must be within six months of your return.
- The return rules cover absences of five months or less. Talk to your DSO before a longer trip.

#### Forms and fees

No USCIS form.
A new visa stamp means a DS-160 application and a consulate appointment.

#### What to watch for

- Since September 2025, the State Department asks visa applicants to apply in their country of nationality or residence. VisaLife has not yet confirmed the current wording on the State Department site.
- On OPT, carry your EAD card and a letter from your employer if you have one.
- If you leave during your 60-day grace period, you cannot come back in F-1 status on that I-20.
