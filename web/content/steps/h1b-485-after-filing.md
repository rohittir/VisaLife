#### What it is

After you file your I-485, USCIS sends a series of notices.

- The receipt notice (I-797C) proves you filed. It gives you no status or benefit.
- A biometrics notice gives you an appointment at an Application Support Center.
- A Request for Evidence (RFE) asks for a document or an answer.
- An interview notice, if the officer decides one is needed.

#### When

Open every notice the day it arrives and write down the deadline.
Check your case with the receipt number on USCIS's Case Status Online.

#### Forms and fees

No new form and no new fee.
Report a change of address within 10 days. Your online account counts.

#### What to watch for

- Missing biometrics without asking to reschedule first can lead USCIS to deny your case as abandoned.
- An RFE gives you at most 12 weeks, and USCIS may not extend it. No answer can mean denial.
- Since Aug. 5, 2026, USCIS may deny without sending an RFE when initial evidence is missing. File a complete package.
- USCIS decides interviews case by case. Employment-based cases are not on its list of automatic waivers.
- A May 2026 USCIS memo reminds officers that adjustment of status is a matter of discretion.
