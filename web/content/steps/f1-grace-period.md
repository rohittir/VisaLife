#### What it is

After you finish your program and any OPT, you have 60 days to prepare to leave, transfer, or change status.
You cannot work during the grace period.

#### When

- It starts after your program end date, or after your OPT or STEM OPT ends.
- If your DSO approved a withdrawal from classes, you get 15 days instead.
- If you fell out of status, you get no grace period.

#### Forms and fees

None for the grace period itself.

#### What to watch for

- If you leave the US during the grace period, you cannot come back in F-1 status on that I-20.
- The paused fixed-period rule would cut this to 30 days. A court order has it on hold as of this review.
