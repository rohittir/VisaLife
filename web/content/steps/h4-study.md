#### What it is

As the spouse or child of an H-1B worker, you can attend school without changing your status.
ICE guidance says this study can be part-time or full-time.

#### When

Any time you are in valid H-4 status.

#### Forms and fees

No immigration form is needed to study on H-4.

#### What to watch for

- Studying does not extend your stay. Your I-94 still follows your spouse's H-1B.
- Student work options such as CPT and OPT belong to F-1 status, not H-4.
- Children lose H-4 status at 21. A child who wants to keep studying usually needs to change to F-1 first.
