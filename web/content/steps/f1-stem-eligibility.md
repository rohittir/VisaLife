#### What it is

The STEM OPT extension adds 24 months of work authorization.
You qualify if your degree is on the STEM Designated Degree Program List and your employer is enrolled in E-Verify.
In some cases you can use an earlier US STEM degree instead.

#### When

- File up to 90 days before your OPT EAD expires.
- File within 60 days of your DSO's STEM OPT recommendation.
- You must file while your current OPT is still valid.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-983 training plan, signed by you and your employer, goes to your DSO.
- Form I-765, category (c)(3)(C): $470 online or $520 on paper.
- Premium processing is optional: $1,780.

#### What to watch for

- If you file on time and your EAD expires while the case is pending, your work authorization continues for up to 180 days, or until USCIS decides.
- The October 2025 rule that ended other automatic EAD extensions did not change this STEM OPT rule.
- Each degree can support only one STEM OPT extension.
