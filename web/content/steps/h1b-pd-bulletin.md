#### What it is

The Visa Bulletin is a monthly State Department publication, prepared with DHS.
For each green card category and country it lists a cut-off date.
A visa is available to you when your priority date is earlier than the cut-off date for your category and country.

#### When

A new bulletin comes out every month. Check it for your category (EB-2 or EB-3) and the India column.
Your priority date is on your I-140 approval notice.

#### Forms and fees

No form. VisaLife's Home priority-date card reads the bulletin for you and shows where your date stands this month.

#### What to watch for

- "C" means current: visas are available to all qualified applicants. "U" means unavailable.
- You are counted in the column for your country of birth, not your citizenship.
- Each month shows two charts, Final Action Dates and Dates for Filing. The next step explains which one you use.
- Cut-off dates can stay still for months or move back. Moving back is called retrogression.
- A new fiscal year on Oct. 1 usually, but not always, restores earlier dates.
