#### What it is

If your OPT or STEM OPT ends before your H-1B starts, cap-gap can fill the gap.
Your F-1 status and work authorization are extended automatically.

#### When

- The extension runs until April 1 of the H-1B fiscal year, or until the H-1B start date if that is earlier.
- The petition must be filed on time, ask for a change of status, and not be frivolous.

#### Forms and fees

No separate form or fee.

#### What to watch for

- Cap-gap ends if the petition is rejected, denied, revoked or withdrawn, or if the change of status is denied or withdrawn. This applies even if the petition is approved for consular processing.
- You must not have violated your F-1 status.
- Some USCIS pages still say cap-gap ends on Oct. 1. The regulation moved the end date to April 1 in January 2025.
