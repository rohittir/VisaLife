#### What it is

H-4 status does not allow you to work.
You can work only after USCIS approves an employment authorization document (EAD) for you.

#### When

From the day you enter in H-4 status until the start date on your EAD card.

#### Forms and fees

None at this stage.
The H-4 EAD application is covered in the next stage.

#### What to watch for

- Starting a job before you hold the EAD card is unauthorized work.
- Under the regulations, any unauthorized work is a failure to maintain your status.
- That can cause problems years later, including when you apply for a green card.
