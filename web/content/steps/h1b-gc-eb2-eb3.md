#### What it is

EB-2 and EB-3 are two employment-based green card categories. Each has its own line on the Visa Bulletin.

- EB-2 is for an advanced degree or exceptional ability. An advanced degree is a degree above a bachelor's, or a bachelor's plus five years of progressive experience in the field.
- EB-3 is for professionals (a bachelor's degree), skilled workers (at least two years of training or experience) and other workers.

#### When

The category is set when your employer writes the PERM, before it is filed.

#### Forms and fees

No separate form. The I-140 names the category.

#### What to watch for

- The category follows the minimum requirements of the job on the PERM, not the highest degree you hold.
- Your priority date works the same way in both categories. Only the line you wait in differs.
- Ask your employer which category was chosen and why. A person who qualifies for EB-2 can sometimes also be filed in EB-3. See the downgrade step.
