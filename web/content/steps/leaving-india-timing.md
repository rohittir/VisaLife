#### What it is

Your arrival date can change the tax year you become resident, and how many RNOR years you get.

#### When

Before you book your move.

#### Forms and fees

- Tax residence counts days. The tax year runs April 1 to March 31. Reaching 182 days in India within one tax year makes you resident for it.
- The second test, 60 days plus 365 days in the 4 years before, only helps if you spent 365 days or more in India in those 4 years.
- FEMA residence is separate. In the FEMA definition quoted by RBI, a person who comes to India for employment or to settle is resident from arrival, not after 182 days.
- So you can be a non-resident for tax and a resident under FEMA at the same time. That is why bank accounts must change "immediately" on return.

#### What to watch for

- Each tax year you are resident uses up one of the nine non-resident years in the RNOR look-back.
- Arriving late in a tax year can leave you under 182 days in that year. Count your days.
- Keep a record of every day you spend in India and abroad.
- Talk to a CPA before you fix your arrival date. A few weeks can change your tax status for years.
