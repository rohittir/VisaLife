#### What it is

Selection lets your employer file Form I-129 for you.
If you are in the US, the petition usually asks to change your status from F-1 to H-1B.
The other choice is consular processing, where you get an H-1B visa abroad.

#### When

Within the filing period on the selection notice. It starts April 1 and lasts at least 90 days.
H-1B employment usually starts on Oct. 1.

#### Forms and fees

Your employer files and pays for Form I-129.
Premium processing costs $2,965.

#### What to watch for

- Travel abroad while the change of status is pending abandons the change of status part of the petition. You would then need an H-1B visa from abroad.
- When your H-1B starts, switch your visa to H-1B in Me.
