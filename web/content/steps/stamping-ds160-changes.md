#### Can I edit a DS-160 after I submit it?

No. You submit a new DS-160 with the correct answers.
You can fill the new one with answers from the old one, using the old application ID. Check every field before you submit.

#### Which changes are minor?

India lists a renewed passport, a new phone number, a new address, a job change, a small change of travel plans and a change of marital status.
For a minor change, keep your appointment.
Bring the DS-160 confirmation page you booked with and the corrected one to the Visa Application Center and the interview.

#### My passport changed after I booked. What do I do?

That counts as a minor change. Bring both DS-160 confirmation pages.
Update the passport number in your profile on the scheduling site. Do not create a new profile.

#### My DS-160 will be more than a year old on my appointment day.

Submit a new DS-160 and bring both confirmation pages.

#### I, or an agent, booked with a placeholder DS-160 or one for someone else.

You must cancel and book again with the correct DS-160 number.
In your profile, choose "Close Application and Start New Application". It can take one business day to appear.
Cancelling uses up one of your scheduling chances.
If your fee receipt has expired or has been used the maximum number of times, you pay a new fee.
If the option does not appear, send a request through "Feedback/Requests" or call the support centre.

#### What do I bring on the day?

The appointment confirmation showing your current DS-160 number and the newest DS-160 confirmation page.
If you made a minor change, also bring the original confirmation page you booked with.

#### I found a mistake just before the interview.

Submit a corrected DS-160 if you can, bring both confirmation pages and tell the officer what changed.
State warns that you may have to correct the form and reschedule.
In India you get one reschedule, so fix errors as early as you can.
