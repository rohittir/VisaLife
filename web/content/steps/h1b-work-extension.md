#### What it is

Your employer files Form I-129 to extend your H-1B.
It sends your I-94, a letter, your last two pay stubs, your latest W-2 and a current LCA.
Each approval is for up to three years and cannot run past the LCA.

#### When

The earliest filing date is six months before the date you need the extension to start.
An extension generally may be filed only while the old petition is still valid. Do not wait.
Your H-4 family's Form I-539 should go in with it.

#### Forms and fees

Your employer pays the filing fees.
Premium processing costs $2,965 and gets a decision in 15 business days.
Only your employer can file the premium processing request. You may pay for it.

#### What to watch for

- If the extension was filed on time and your status expires, you may keep working for the same employer for up to 240 days past the end date. The 240 days end earlier if USCIS denies it.
- The 240-day rule covers the same employer and the same job. A job change uses portability.
- USCIS says leaving the US does not by itself get a pending extension denied. You will still need a valid visa to return.
- The check on Home counts down to your extension window from the I-797 date in Me.
