#### What it is

PAN is your tax ID in India. Aadhaar is the national ID. Residents have to link the two.

#### When

Before you return, check that your PAN is operative.
Enrol for Aadhaar when you arrive, or earlier if you have an Indian passport.

#### Forms and fees

- Non-residents under the Income-tax Act are exempt from linking PAN with Aadhaar. The Department's page does not say what happens when you become resident. Plan to link.
- A PAN issued on an Aadhaar enrolment ID had to be linked by December 31, 2025. Otherwise it became inoperative from January 1, 2026.
- An inoperative PAN gets no refund and no interest on it, higher TDS and higher TCS. Making it operative costs Rs 1,000.
- Aadhaar for an NRI: UIDAI says an NRI with a valid Indian passport can enrol at any centre without the 182-day condition. The passport and an email address are mandatory, and a foreign address on the passport can be used.
- Aadhaar for a resident needs 182 days or more in India in the 12 months before the application.

#### What to watch for

- The Department's Link Aadhaar page still cites the 1961 Act's section number. Check the current rule.
- Linking Aadhaar is a tax rule. It does not by itself change your tax residence.
- Talk to a CA if your PAN is inoperative and you have a refund due.
