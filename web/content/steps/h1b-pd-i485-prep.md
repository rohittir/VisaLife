#### What it is

The I-485 needs documents that take weeks to collect. Start before your date is current for filing.
Your employment-based checklist includes:

- two passport photos and a government photo ID,
- your birth certificate,
- proof of how you were admitted, such as your I-94,
- the I-797 for your approved I-140, unless it is filed with the I-485,
- proof of lawful status, unless a statutory exception applies,
- the medical exam on Form I-693,
- Supplement J, if you are using job portability.

Your spouse and children file their own I-485 with proof of the relationship.

#### When

Have everything ready the month your date reaches the chart USCIS accepts.
Since Dec. 2, 2024, Form I-693 must be submitted with the I-485, or USCIS may reject it.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-485: $1,440 on paper, $1,390 online. Use the edition listed on the form page.
- Your work permit (I-765) and travel document (I-131) are separate forms with separate fees. See the work permit step.
- Form I-693: USCIS charges nothing. The civil surgeon sets the exam fee. Find one on USCIS's doctor locator.
- The I-693 goes in a sealed envelope. A medical signed after Nov. 1, 2023 supports only the I-485 it is filed with.

#### What to watch for

- Section 245(k) forgives up to 180 days of status violation or unauthorized work for employment-based applicants who were lawfully admitted. A longer gap can block the I-485.
- Every family member uses your priority date and category. Children are protected from aging out only by CSPA. See the child turning 21 step.
- USCIS changed the I-485 edition and filing location in autumn 2026, and rejects old editions. Check the form page the day you file.
