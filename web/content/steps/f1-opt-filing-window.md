#### What it is

Post-completion OPT lets you work for up to 12 months in a job related to your major.
Each degree level gets its own 12 months.
Any pre-completion OPT you used is subtracted.

#### When

- First, your DSO recommends you for OPT in SEVIS and gives you a new I-20.
- File Form I-765 within 30 days of that recommendation.
- The filing window runs from 90 days before to 60 days after your program end date.
- Your requested start date can be no later than 60 days after the program end date.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-765, category (c)(3)(B): $470 online or $520 on paper.
- Premium processing is optional: $1,780 for a decision within 30 business days.

#### What to watch for

- Both deadlines are firm. A late application is denied.
- USCIS changed the I-765 mailing address on Oct. 7, 2026. Filing online avoids the address question.
