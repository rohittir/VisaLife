#### What it is

California, New Jersey and New York each ask for a return that covers the part of the year you lived there.
They tax the income you earned while you were a resident.
Other states have their own rules, forms and limits.

#### When

Together with your federal return for the year you leave.

#### Forms and fees

- California: a part-year resident files Form 540NR. California taxes all your income while you lived there, and California-source income after.
- New Jersey: file the resident return NJ-1040 with your dates of residence. File an NJ-1040NR for New Jersey income after you move.
- New York: file Form IT-203, the part-year resident return.
- Any other state: use your state tax agency. The IRS page in the sources links to each one.

#### What to watch for

- Moving away does not end California tax on California-source work. California also treats some stock pay as California-source.
- We read the pages for three states only. Check your own state's tax agency.
- Talk to a CPA if you lived in more than one state this year.
