#### What it is

- Final Action Dates show when a green card can be approved.
- Dates for Filing show an earlier date, so you can file your I-485 before approval is possible.

USCIS, not the State Department, decides which chart you may use to file your I-485 each month.

#### When

Each month, after the bulletin comes out, open USCIS's "Adjustment of Status Filing Charts" page.
USCIS links the chart it chose within about a week of the bulletin.
If your priority date is earlier than the date for your category and India on that chart, you may file.

#### Forms and fees

No form. If the page says to use Dates for Filing and you are eligible, file Form I-485 then.

#### What to watch for

- Filing and approval are separate. USCIS can approve your I-485 only when your priority date is earlier than the Final Action Date, or the category is current.
- If a category is current on Final Action Dates, or its Final Action date is later than its Dates for Filing date, you may file using Final Action Dates.
- Filing early gets you a pending I-485, which opens the work permit and travel document, and job portability after 180 days.
- For a child's CSPA age, USCIS uses the Final Action Dates chart for I-485s filed on or after Aug. 15, 2025, whichever chart you filed under.
