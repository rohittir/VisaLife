#### What it is

A Social Security number (SSN) is the number your employer, bank and tax filing use.
SSA treats H-1B as a status that may work, so you can get an SSN.
SSA's public pages blocked VisaLife's tools. This step comes from SSA's operating manual, so confirm the details with SSA.

#### When

After you arrive and your entry record exists.
SSA checks your immigration documents with DHS. If your entry is very recent, it may not be there yet.
No SSA page VisaLife could read sets a waiting period.

#### Forms and fees

Apply at a local SSA office with Form SS-5.
Bring your passport and your I-94 printed from the CBP website. SSA lists the I-94 with an unexpired passport as the evidence of H-1B status.
Take your I-797 as well. SSA does not list it as stand-alone evidence of status.

#### What to watch for

- SSA does not charge for an SSN. Do not pay anyone to get one for you.
- Your H-4 spouse generally needs an H-4 EAD first. SSA treats H-4 alone as not allowed to work.
