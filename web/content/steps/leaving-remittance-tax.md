#### What it is

Since January 1, 2026, a 1% federal tax applies to some money transfers sent abroad.
You owe it as the sender. The company that handles the transfer collects it.

#### When

Each time you send money abroad by a method that is taxed.

#### Forms and fees

- The tax applies when you hand over cash, a money order, a cashier's check or a similar physical instrument.
- It does not apply when the money comes out of an account at a US-regulated financial institution, or is paid with a debit or credit card issued in the US.
- The provider collects the tax and reports it on its own Form 720.

#### What to watch for

- The IRS has only proposed the detailed rules, in April 2026. The proposal would count the amount that reaches the recipient and leave out the provider's fees.
- Under the proposal, a check that the provider cashes for you counts as cash.
- Send money from your bank account or with a US card, and check the provider's receipt.
- Talk to a CPA if you send large sums from the US in the year you leave.
