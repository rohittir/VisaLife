#### What it is

A new H-1B or H-4 stamp means a visa interview at a US consulate, with a new DS-160 application.
Rules changed in 2025 and 2026, and VisaLife could read these State Department pages only on another State Department host. Confirm appointment availability for India on the US Embassy and consulate site.

#### When

Before the stamp you rely on expires, with time to spare. Check State's wait-time page for your consulate.

#### Forms and fees

- Where: State asks applicants to apply in their country of nationality or residence. Applying elsewhere "might find that it will be more difficult to qualify", waits can be longer, and fees are not refunded or transferred.
- Interview: the list of people who can still skip the interview, effective Oct. 1, 2025, does not include H-1B or H-4. Plan for an in-person interview.
- Online presence: since Dec. 15, 2025, H-1B and H-4 applicants must set their social media profiles to "public".
- Fees: the visa application fee for H-1B is $205 and is not refunded. India charges no extra issuance fee for H-1B or H-4. The July 2025 law adds a $250 visa integrity fee due at issuance. VisaLife found no State Department notice that collection has started.

#### What to watch for

- State discourages applying in a third country such as Canada, Mexico or a European country. Do not plan around it.
- An officer may send your case to administrative processing (section 221(g)). State gives no timeline. If you do not supply what is asked within a year, you reapply and pay again.
- There is no in-US renewal program for H-1B. The only one was a pilot in early 2024.
- A new stamp for a new petition needs that petition approved first.
