#### What it is

Leaving the US without advance parole generally abandons your I-485.
There are two safe ways to travel.

- Hold a valid H-1B or H-4 status, stay eligible to resume the job, and return on a valid visa stamp if you need one.
- Hold a valid advance parole document, and be inspected and paroled on return.

#### When

Before every trip, check which route you will use.
If your stamp is expired, you need a new one or advance parole before you go.

#### Forms and fees

Advance parole is Form I-131. See the work permit step for the fee.
Do not leave on the strength of a pending I-131. Wait for the approved document.

#### What to watch for

- Advance parole does not guarantee you will be allowed in. CBP decides.
- No USCIS page says what status you hold if you return on advance parole instead of on an H-1B visa. Ask counsel before choosing it.
- On Aug. 13, 2026 the Board of Immigration Appeals ruled that leaving on advance parole counts as a departure for the unlawful-presence bar. It matters only if you built up more than 180 days of unlawful presence.
- The $100,000 payment does not stop existing H-1B visa holders from travelling.
- A trip to India can take longer than you plan if your visa stamping goes to administrative processing.
