#### What it is

Your spouse and your unmarried children under 21 can be admitted in H-4 status.
Their status follows yours. If your H-1B status ends, theirs ends with it.
H-4 family members can study. They cannot work without an H-4 EAD.

#### When

Each person is admitted on their own H-4 visa stamp and gets their own I-94.
Check that each family member's I-94 date matches yours.

#### Forms and fees

Your H-4 family's status does not extend by itself when yours does.
Each person extends with Form I-539, usually filed together with your employer's H-1B extension. Children are added with Form I-539A.
The fee is $470 on paper and $420 online.

#### What to watch for

- File the family's I-539 before their I-94 expires.
- A family member who holds an H-4 EAD should file its renewal at the same time.
- Your spouse's eligibility for work opens when your I-140 is approved. See the H-4 EAD step.
- A child loses H-4 status at 21. Plan for it early.
- Your spouse can follow VisaLife's H-4 journey with their own profile.
