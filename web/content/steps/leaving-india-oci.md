#### What it is

A child born in the US to an Indian parent is a US citizen. India does not allow Indian citizenship together with another citizenship.
For a minor with foreign citizenship, the official route is an OCI card. It is not citizenship.
Under section 7A of the Citizenship Act, a minor whose parents or one parent is an Indian citizen is eligible.

#### When

After the child has a US passport.

#### Forms and fees

- Apply online at ociservices.gov.in. The portal FAQ lists US$275 per applicant.
- Documents for a minor include the child's birth certificate, the parents' marriage certificate, proof of a parent's Indian citizenship, the child's present passport, and address proof.
- The portal FAQ says about 30 days after acknowledgement.
- A birth abroad can also be registered at an Indian consulate within one year, for citizenship by descent under section 4. The parents must declare that the child holds no other country's passport.

#### What to watch for

- The fee and documents have changed before. Confirm them on the live application.
- The US requires a US citizen child to use a US passport at its borders. India asks for a declaration of no foreign passport when it registers a birth. The pages we read do not say how families reconcile the two. Ask the consulate.
- We could not read the consulate's birth registration page, or the application centre's checklist.
- Talk to an immigration attorney before you register a birth.
