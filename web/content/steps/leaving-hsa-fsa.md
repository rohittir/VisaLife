#### What it is

An HSA is yours. It stays with you when you change jobs or leave the US.
A health FSA belongs to your employer's plan. Its rules for the end of a job are in the plan document.

#### When

Before your last day. Check your FSA plan's cut-off date and its deadline for claims.

#### Forms and fees

- HSA money is tax-free for qualified medical expenses at any time.
- Other withdrawals are taxed as income. Before 65 they also carry an extra 20% tax. That extra tax does not apply after disability, age 65 or death.
- You can add to an HSA only in months when you have a high-deductible health plan on the first day. The 2026 limits are $4,400 for self-only cover and $8,750 for family cover.
- Report HSA payouts on Form 8889. It can go with Form 1040-NR.
- You can use HSA money to pay COBRA premiums.
- A health FSA lets you set aside up to $3,400 for 2026. A plan can carry over up to $680, or give a grace period of up to 2½ months. It cannot offer both.

#### What to watch for

- Unspent FSA money is generally lost at the end of the plan year. What happens when your job ends is set by the plan, so ask.
- Pub 969 does not say whether a non-resident can keep adding to an HSA, or how care you receive abroad is treated. Ask your HSA provider.
- Talk to a CPA before you withdraw HSA money for something you are not sure qualifies.
