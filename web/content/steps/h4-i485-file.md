#### What it is

When your spouse's priority date is current, you can apply for your own green card as a derivative.
Each family member files a separate Form I-485.
Children under 21 can file too.

#### When

When the priority date is current under the Visa Bulletin chart USCIS says to use that month.
You can file together with your spouse.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-485: $1,440 on paper, $1,390 online.
- A child under 14 filing with a parent: $950 on paper, $900 online.

#### What to watch for

- Your case depends on your spouse's petition and priority date.
- For a child close to 21, see "Children and CSPA age" below.
