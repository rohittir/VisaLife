#### What it is

Employer health cover usually ends when your job ends.
The end date can be your last day or the end of that month. It depends on the plan.

#### When

Ask HR for the exact end date before your last day.
Then plan how you will be covered from that date until you leave.

#### Forms and fees

COBRA lets you keep your employer's plan for a while, at your own cost.

- You have 60 days to elect it. The clock starts on the later of the day cover ends and the day the plan gives you the notice.
- It can last up to 18 months after a job ends.
- You usually pay the full premium plus 2%.
- The first payment is due within 45 days after you elect. After that you have a 30-day grace period for each payment.
- Once you elect and pay, cover reaches back to the day your job cover ended.
- COBRA generally applies to employers with 20 or more employees.

The Marketplace is the other option. Losing job cover opens a special enrollment period of 60 days before and after the loss.
Choosing to stop paying for COBRA does not qualify.

#### What to watch for

- To enroll in a Marketplace plan you must live in the US. Its plans pay for care from US providers.
- None of the official pages we read say that COBRA or a Marketplace plan pays for care outside the US. Ask the plan before you rely on it abroad.
- A gap between the day cover ends and the day you leave is the main risk. Check both dates.
- HSA money can pay COBRA premiums.
- Talk to your plan administrator or a benefits adviser about your exact dates.
