#### What it is

H-4 is the status for the spouse and unmarried children under 21 of an H-1B worker.
You are admitted for the same period as your spouse.
If your spouse's H-1B status ends, your H-4 status ends with it.

#### When

This applies for as long as you hold H-4 status.
Each time you enter the US, CBP creates a new I-94 record for you.

#### Forms and fees

No form is needed to hold H-4 status day to day.
Your I-94 shows your class (H-4) and your "Admit Until" date.
You can download it free from the CBP I-94 website.

#### What to watch for

- Your I-94 date, not your visa stamp, controls how long you can stay.
- Check that your I-94 date matches your spouse's.
- Keep copies of every I-797 approval notice for you and your spouse.
