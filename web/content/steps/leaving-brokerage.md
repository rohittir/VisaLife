#### What it is

A brokerage account can often stay open after you leave. That is each broker's own policy, and policies differ.
For tax, you become a non-resident alien. That changes the forms you give the broker and what it withholds.

#### When

Before you leave, ask your broker three things: can you keep the account, can you keep buying, and what does it need from you.

#### Forms and fees

- A foreign individual gives the payer Form W-8BEN. It shows you are not a US person and claims any treaty rate.
- Form W-8BEN is valid from the date you sign until the end of the third following calendar year. If you sign in 2026, it is valid to December 31, 2029.
- You must tell the payer within 30 days if your facts change, for example a new US address or becoming a US resident again.

#### What to watch for

- Tax rules do not decide whether a broker keeps your account open. The broker does.
- Your US stocks can face US estate tax even if you live in India. A return is needed if your US-situated assets go over $60,000. India is not on the IRS list of estate tax treaty countries.
- Talk to a CPA before you move your account or change brokers.
