#### What it is

With an I-485 pending, you can apply for an EAD and for advance parole, a travel document.
This EAD is category (c)(9), not (c)(26).

#### When

With your I-485, or any time while it is pending.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-765, category (c)(9): $260 if you paid the fee on an I-485 filed on or after April 1, 2024.
- Form I-131 for advance parole: $630 on paper, $580 online.

#### What to watch for

- Renewals filed on or after Oct. 30, 2025 get no automatic extension. Renew early.
- Leaving the US without advance parole generally abandons your I-485. The next step explains the H-4 exception.
