#### What it is

H-1B status is limited to six years in total.
Your H-1B and L-1 time are added together. Time in H-4 does not count.
Days you spent outside the US during an approved H-1B can be recaptured, so you can use them later.

#### When

Count your time well before year six.
You can ask for recaptured time in a later H-1B petition, at any time before you use the full period.

#### Forms and fees

Your employer asks for the recaptured time in the H-1B petition and has to show the exact number of days.
Evidence includes passport stamps, I-94 travel history, airline tickets or boarding passes, and a chart of your dates.
USCIS may grant all, part or none of the request.

#### What to watch for

- Only time physically outside the US counts, and each absence must exceed 24 hours.
- After one full year outside the US, you can start a new six-year period. A new cap-subject petition would need a cap number.
- If you reach six years while waiting for a green card, see the step on extending past six years.
- Keep a travel log. You can get your travel history from CBP's I-94 website.
