#### What it is

You can sell your US investments before you leave, or hold them.
Each choice changes what is taxed, when and where. VisaLife cannot tell you which one suits you.

#### When

Before your residency end date, if you are thinking of selling.
Until that date you are taxed as a US resident, on gains from all sources.
After it, the non-resident rules apply to your US income. The next step lists the rates.

#### Forms and fees

Questions that change the answer:

- Will your broker let you keep the account and keep buying?
- Is the sale before or after your residency end date?
- Do you still have a US tax home, or have you moved it abroad?
- How will your new country tax the same holdings? For India, see the steps on RNOR status and what India taxes.
- Do you hold retirement accounts as well? They follow their own rules, in the steps on your 401(k) and IRA.

#### What to watch for

- The year you leave is split in two. IRS guidance does not spell out how gains are treated between the day you leave and December 31. Ask a CPA before you sell in that window.
- VisaLife covers the US side. If you move to a country other than India, that country taxes you under its own rules.
- Talk to a CPA before you sell large positions or move holdings between accounts.
