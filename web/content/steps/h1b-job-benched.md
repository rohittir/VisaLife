#### What it is

Your employer must keep paying your required wage while you have no project.
Time without work because of the employer's decision, such as no work assigned, is paid time.
This is often called benching.

#### When

From your H-1B start date until the employment relationship ends.
The pay duty ends at a bona fide termination. The employer notifies USCIS and offers your return transportation where required.

#### Forms and fees

If you think you are owed pay, DOL says to send Form WH-4 to your nearest Wage and Hour Division office.
You can also call 1-866-487-9243.
There is no fee.

#### What to watch for

- A complaint must be filed within 12 months of the latest date of the violation.
- Salaried workers get their full pay. Hourly workers get a full-time week at the wage on the LCA.
- Pay is not required for time off at your own request, such as travel or caring for a relative. It is also not required when you cannot work, such as after an accident, unless a benefit plan or law requires it.
- If the LCA says part-time, your employer must pay at least the hours on the petition.
- Your employer may not retaliate against you for complaining.
