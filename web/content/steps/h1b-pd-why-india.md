#### What it is

Two limits in the law create the line.

- About 140,000 employment-based green cards are available worldwide each year (INA 201(d)). Each of EB-1, EB-2 and EB-3 gets up to 28.6% of that number, plus unused numbers from higher categories.
- No single country may receive more than 7% of the combined family and employment limits (INA 202(a)(2)).

#### When

These limits apply every year.
USCIS names India among the countries over the 7% limit, which is why Indian-born applicants have their own, much longer line.

#### Forms and fees

No form. Your priority date is your place in the line.

#### What to watch for

- Your spouse and children use numbers from the same limit when they become permanent residents. A family is not a single number.
- The 7% limit relaxes when a category has unused visas in a quarter (INA 202(a)(5)). That is one reason dates sometimes jump.
- Congress has considered bills that change per-country limits. See the Policy tab. Nothing changes your place in line until a bill becomes law.
- VisaLife does not predict your date. The Home card shows the pace over the last 12 months, which is not a forecast.
