#### What it is

Renewals filed on or after Oct. 30, 2025 no longer get an automatic extension while they are pending.
Your permission to work ends after the expiry date on your current card.
Before this DHS rule, a timely renewal could keep your card valid for up to 540 days.

#### When

You can file up to 180 days before your current EAD expires.
If your I-94 also needs extending, USCIS decides the I-539 before the EAD.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-765, category (c)(26): $520 on paper.
- Form I-539 for the H-4 extension, if needed: $470 on paper.

#### What to watch for

- Plan for a gap in work if the new card arrives late. Tell your employer early.
- Renewals filed before Oct. 30, 2025 keep the older automatic extension rules.
- The change came from an interim final rule. Check the USCIS page for any later change.
