#### What it is

Your visa stamp is an entry document. Your I-94 date, not the stamp, says how long you may stay.
An expired stamp does not end your status inside the US. You only need a valid one to come back.

#### When

Before you book. Check three dates: the visa stamp, your passport and your I-94.

#### Forms and fees

No form is needed to leave and return on a valid stamp.
Carry your passport with the visa, your latest I-797 and, if a new petition is pending after a job change, its filing receipt.
A short trip of 30 days or less to Canada, Mexico or an adjacent island can use automatic visa revalidation with an expired stamp. You must not have applied for a new visa while away, and your I-94 must still be valid.

#### What to watch for

- India is on CBP's list of countries exempt from the six-month passport rule. Your passport needs to be valid only for your stay, and it still cannot run past its printed expiry date.
- If your stamp is expired, you need a new one before you return from anywhere except those short neighbour trips. See the stamping step.
- USCIS says leaving the US does not by itself get a pending extension denied. Ask your employer's lawyer before you travel with one pending.
- With a pending I-485, read the I-485 travel step first.
- After you return, check your new I-94 on the CBP website.
