#### What it is

You apply on Form I-765 under category (c)(26).
You can file it on its own, with your I-539 extension, or with the I-539 and your spouse's H-1B petition.

#### When

File once you are eligible.
When you file it with an I-539, USCIS decides the EAD only after it decides the I-539.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-765: $520. The (c)(26) category cannot be filed online, so you file on paper.
- Evidence: proof of your H-4 status, a photo ID, your marriage certificate, proof of your spouse's approved I-140 or AC21 extension, and two passport-style photos.
- USCIS changed the I-765 filing address on Oct. 7, 2026. Check the current address before you mail.

#### What to watch for

- Your EAD can last only as long as your H-4 I-94. File it with each H-4 extension to keep them in step.
- You can start work only on or after the start date on the card.
- If you are filing an I-485 at the same time, use category (c)(9) instead. Filing under (c)(26) can get the I-485 rejected.
