#### What it is

F-1 status requires a full course of study every term.
For most undergraduates, that is at least 12 credit hours a term.
For graduate students, your school sets what counts as full time.

#### When

Every term except your annual vacation.

#### Forms and fees

No USCIS form or fee.
A reduced course load needs your DSO's approval before you drop below full time.

#### What to watch for

- Only one online class, or three credits, per term counts toward your full load.
- Dropping below full time without DSO approval puts you out of status.
