#### What it is

The Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers Disclosure Scheme, 2026 lets some people declare foreign assets or foreign income they did not report. It is in Chapter IV of the Finance Act 2026.
The portal calls it FAST-DS or FADS 2026.

#### When

The income tax portal says the window runs from August 16 to December 31, 2026.
That date can change. Check the portal before you rely on it.

#### Forms and fees

- Who can use it: a person who was resident in the relevant year, or a non-resident or RNOR who was resident in the year the income arose or the asset was acquired.
- Category 1: an undisclosed asset or income up to Rs 1 crore. You pay 30% of the asset's value on March 31, 2026, plus 30% of the income, plus an equal amount again. That is 60% of the total.
- Category 2: a foreign asset bought from income earned abroad while you were non-resident, and not reported in your return after you became resident. Up to Rs 5 crore. The fee is Rs 1 lakh.
- Declare on Form 1 on the portal. Payment goes through a challan.

#### What to watch for

- Nothing you pay is refundable, and a wrong payment cannot be corrected.
- The scheme covers years up to March 31, 2026. It gives immunity under the Black Money Act for what you declare.
- The scheme is built on the 1961 Act's rules for who is resident. Whether your case fits is a CPA question.
- We read the Act and the portal. We did not read the Government's notification that sets the dates.
- Talk to a CPA before you file Form 1. A declaration cannot be undone.
