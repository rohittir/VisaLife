#### What it is

H-4 is only for unmarried children under 21.
A child loses H-4 status at 21.
A child who wants to stay usually needs a status of their own, most often F-1.

#### When

The change of status must be filed while the child's H-4 status is still valid.

#### Forms and fees

- Change to F-1 inside the US: a Form I-20 from a school, then Form I-539 ($470 on paper, $420 online).
- Premium processing for a change to F-1 costs $2,075.
- If the child is in a pending I-485, CSPA may protect them. See "Children and CSPA age".

#### What to watch for

- The application must reach USCIS before the child's H-4 status ends.
- Studying on H-4 does not extend H-4 past 21.
