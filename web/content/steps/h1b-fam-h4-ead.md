#### What it is

An H-4 EAD is a work permit for your H-4 spouse. It lets them work for any employer.
They qualify as soon as one of these is true:

- you are the main beneficiary of an approved I-140, or
- you have H-1B time beyond six years under AC21 sections 106(a) and (b).

#### When

Your spouse can apply the day you meet one condition. Many families wait years without knowing they qualify.

#### Forms and fees

Your spouse files Form I-765, category (c)(26), on paper. The fee is $520.
They include proof of H-4 status, a photo ID, your marriage certificate and proof of your I-140 approval or AC21 extension.

#### What to watch for

- H-4 children cannot get this EAD. Only spouses qualify.
- Your spouse cannot work until the card's start date.
- Renewals filed on or after Oct. 30, 2025 get no automatic extension. File up to 180 days before expiry.
- DHS lists a plan to end H-4 EADs on its regulatory agenda. No proposed rule has been published, and eligibility is unchanged.
- Your spouse can follow VisaLife's H-4 journey with their own profile.
