#### What it is

You can apply for citizenship after five years as a permanent resident.
It is three years if you have been married to and living with a US citizen for that whole period.
You also need 30 months of physical presence in the US and three months living in your state or district.

#### When

The clock starts on the "resident since" date on your card. Time on H-1B does not count.
You can file up to 90 days before you complete the residence period.

#### Forms and fees

Form N-400: $760 on paper, $710 online.
A reduced fee of $380 is available on paper if your income is at or below 400% of the federal poverty guideline.

#### What to watch for

- An absence of more than six months and less than one year is presumed to break your continuous residence, unless you can show otherwise.
- An absence of one year or more breaks it.
- Count your days outside the US before you file.
- Check the N-400 page for the current civics test and form edition.
