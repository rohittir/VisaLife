#### What it is

To come back after a trip abroad, you generally need a valid H-4 visa stamp in your passport.
Your spouse's H-1B status must also still be valid.

#### When

Before you book any international trip.

#### Forms and fees

- A new visa stamp means a DS-160 application and a consulate appointment.
- Since September 2025, the State Department asks visa applicants to apply in their country of nationality or residence.
- In India, H-4 renewals do not qualify for an interview waiver. Plan for an interview.

#### What to watch for

- Leaving the US while a change of status is pending generally abandons it. A pending I-539 extension is usually treated the same way.
- From December 2025, the State Department reviews the online presence of H-1B and H-4 visa applicants. VisaLife has not yet confirmed the details on the State Department site.
- After you return, check your new I-94 on the CBP website.
