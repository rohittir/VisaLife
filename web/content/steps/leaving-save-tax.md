#### What it is

Most of the tax you can save as a leaver comes from a few choices before you go, and one return after.

#### When

Before you take money out of a retirement account.
Then again in the spring after you leave, when you file.

#### Forms and fees

- Roll over instead of cashing out. A direct rollover has no withholding, no income and no 10% tax.
- Moving abroad is not an exception to the 10% additional tax before age 59½.
- Give each payer a Form W-8BEN, so it withholds the treaty rate instead of 30%.
- File Form 1040-NR to get back tax that was withheld too high. A refund claim is due 3 years after the return was filed or 2 years after the tax was paid, whichever is later.
- To claim deductions and credits, file within 16 months of the due date.
- If your only reason to file is a refund of tax over-withheld on dividends or interest, a simplified Form 1040-NR route exists.
- Report treaty benefits on Form 1040-NR, Schedule OI, and attach any Form 1042-S.

#### What to watch for

- Form 1040-C at departure does not replace the annual return.
- Plan withholding does not cover the 10% additional tax. You may owe it with your return.
- A late return can lose you deductions and credits, though not the credit for tax withheld.
- Talk to a CPA before you file the final return. It is the one place where small mistakes cost real money.
