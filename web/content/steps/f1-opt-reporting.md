#### What it is

On OPT, your school still keeps your SEVIS record, and you must keep it up to date.
You can report through the SEVP Portal or through your DSO.

#### When

Report a change of name, address or employment within 10 days.

#### Forms and fees

No form or fee.
The SEVP Portal is for students on post-completion OPT.

#### What to watch for

- A SEVIS record with no employer counts as unemployment, even if you are working.
- Employer dates outside your OPT dates also count as unemployment.
