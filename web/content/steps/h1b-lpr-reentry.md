#### What it is

Your green card alone lets you return after a trip of less than a year.
For a longer stay abroad, apply for a re-entry permit before you leave.
A permit is valid for up to two years, or one year if you spent more than four of the last five years abroad. It cannot be extended.

#### When

Apply before you leave the US.
You must be physically in the US when you file, and again for your biometrics appointment.

#### Forms and fees

Form I-131 on paper: $630. Re-entry permits cannot be filed online.
Renew your green card with Form I-90 ($465 on paper, $415 online) once it has expired or expires within six months.

#### What to watch for

- Over one year abroad is a general guide for abandoning your permanent residence. Shorter trips can count too, if you moved your life abroad.
- After more than 180 days abroad, CBP may treat you as seeking admission rather than returning.
- If you are abroad too long without a permit, you may be able to seek a returning resident visa (SB-1). The standard is strict: you must show the delay was beyond your control.
- Long absences also break the naturalization clock.
