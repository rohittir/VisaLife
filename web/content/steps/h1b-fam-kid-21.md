#### What it is

Two things end for a child at 21, and the Child Status Protection Act (CSPA) helps with only one.

- H-4 status ends at 21. CSPA does not protect it.
- A green card as your derivative needs the child to be under 21. CSPA can lower the age that counts.

#### When

Plan from age 20. Both fixes must be in place before the birthday.
CSPA age is the child's age when a visa became available, minus the time your I-140 was pending.
The child must seek the green card within one year of that date, for example by filing an I-485.

#### Forms and fees

To keep a status after 21, the usual route is F-1.

- The child is accepted by a SEVP-certified school and receives a Form I-20.
- The child pays the I-901 SEVIS fee.
- The child files Form I-539 before H-4 status ends. USCIS suggests at least 45 days ahead.

The fee is $470 on paper and $420 online.

#### What to watch for

- Do not start classes until USCIS approves the change of status.
- For I-485s filed on or after Aug. 15, 2025, USCIS uses the Final Action Dates chart to find the date a visa became available. A child can age out even if the family filed under Dates for Filing.
- If a child still ages out, the petition converts to the matching category and keeps your original priority date (INA 203(h)(3)).
- This decision is strategic. Talk to an immigration attorney.
