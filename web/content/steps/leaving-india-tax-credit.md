#### What it is

If India taxes income that the US also taxed, you can claim credit in India for the US tax. This is the foreign tax credit.

#### When

In the return for the year you offer the income to tax in India.
File the claim within 12 months of the end of that tax year, and only if your return was filed in time.

#### Forms and fees

- Form 44, under Rule 76 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026. From tax year 2026-27 it takes the place of Form 67. Returns for earlier years still use Form 67.
- Attach a certificate or statement from the US tax authority or the payer, or a statement you sign, with proof of the tax paid.
- The credit is the lower of the Indian tax on that income and the foreign tax paid. Foreign tax above the treaty rate is ignored.
- If the foreign tax for the year is Rs 1 lakh or more, an accountant must verify the form.
- Article 25 of the India-US treaty lets India allow a deduction from Indian tax equal to the US tax paid, up to the Indian tax on that income. The credit is claimed under section 159 of the Act.

#### What to watch for

- Credit comes in the year the income is taxed in India. If the foreign tax is later refunded, Form 44 is needed again.
- Rule 76 was read as notified on March 20, 2026. The Rules were amended later, so check the current text.
- ITR-2 or ITR-3 carries Schedules FSI and TR with the claim.
- Talk to a CA before you claim credit. Mistakes in the order of US and Indian tax are hard to undo.
