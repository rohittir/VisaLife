#### What it is

Your family's status follows yours.

- H-4 spouse and children are in status as long as the H-1B holder is. They share the 60 days and have no separate clock.
- F-2 family depend on the F-1 student's status.
- A child born in the US is a US citizen. The Supreme Court decided this on June 30, 2026.

#### When

Plan the family's last day and travel dates together.
If a baby born in the US has no passport yet, apply before you leave.

#### Forms and fees

- A US passport for a child under 16 is Form DS-11, in person, with both parents. If one parent cannot come, Form DS-3053 and more documents are needed.
- The State page lists $100 for the passport book plus a $35 acceptance fee. A child's passport is valid for 5 years.
- Bring the child's US birth certificate and parent ID with photocopies.

#### What to watch for

- A US citizen must enter and leave the US on a US passport, even if the child has another nationality.
- India does not allow dual citizenship. For the India side, see the step on OCI for children born in the US.
- An executive order of August 2026 limits citizenship documents for some births. H-1B, H-4 and F-1 are not among its listed categories, and it is being challenged. Check State's passport page for the current rule.
- Talk to an immigration attorney about a family member with their own case pending.
