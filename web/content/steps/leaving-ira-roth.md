#### What it is

An IRA stays yours when you leave. You do not have to close it.
Traditional IRA withdrawals are taxed as income. Roth IRA withdrawals can be tax-free once they qualify.

#### When

Before you go, ask your IRA custodian what it needs once your address is outside the US.
A foreign person gives the payer a Form W-8BEN. Ask when the custodian wants yours.

#### Forms and fees

- A traditional IRA payment to a non-resident alien is withheld at 30% or the treaty rate. Even if you ask for no withholding, the payer must still withhold at the non-resident rate.
- India's 0% treaty rate is for pensions and annuities paid as periodic payments. It does not cover a lump sum.
- A Roth IRA payout is qualified when the five-year period that starts with your first Roth IRA contribution has ended and you are 59½, disabled or have died, or the money buys a first home (up to $10,000 for life).
- Other Roth money comes out in a set order: your contributions first, then conversions, then earnings.
- The 10% additional tax can apply to payouts before 59½. Moving abroad is not an exception.
- Form 5329 reports that tax. It can go with your Form 1040-NR.

#### What to watch for

- You can add to an IRA only from taxable compensation for the year.
- No IRS page we read says whether a qualified Roth payout to someone living abroad is treated differently. Ask the custodian how it will report and withhold on yours.
- You can roll over from one IRA to another once in 12 months. A direct transfer between custodians does not count toward that limit.
- Talk to a CPA before you withdraw. The tax depends on how the payment is classified, your residency end date and the treaty.
