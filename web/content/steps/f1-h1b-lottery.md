#### What it is

Most first H-1B jobs need a lottery selection.
Your employer registers you online.
Since the FY2027 season, the lottery is weighted by the wage level of the job offer.

#### When

Registration runs for at least 14 days and has recently been held in March.
USCIS announces the dates each year.
Selected employers can file the H-1B petition from April 1, within a window of at least 90 days.

#### Forms and fees

- Registration fee: $215, paid by the employer.
- Weighting: Level IV counts four times, Level III three times, Level II twice, Level I once.

#### What to watch for

- If more than one employer registers you, USCIS uses the lowest wage level among them.
- Selection is not approval. Your employer still has to file a petition.
