#### What it is

A September 2025 proclamation added a $100,000 payment for some new H-1B petitions.
It targets petitions for people outside the US without a valid H-1B visa, and petitions that ask for consular notification.

#### When

It covers petitions filed at or after 12:01 a.m. Eastern on Sept. 21, 2025.

#### Forms and fees

- It does not apply when USCIS grants a change of status, extension or amendment inside the US.
- It does apply if USCIS finds you ineligible for the change, for example if you leave the US before the decision.

#### What to watch for

- On June 8, 2026, a federal court vacated the guidance that puts the payment in place. An appeals court refused to pause that order on July 24, 2026.
- DHS says it will collect the payment if the order is lifted. Check the USCIS H-1B page before your employer files.
