#### What it is

Your employer files a Labor Condition Application (LCA) with the Department of Labor before your petition.
It promises you at least the greater of two wages: the actual wage the employer pays similar workers, or the prevailing wage for the job and area.

#### When

Your employer must give you a copy of the certified LCA no later than the day you report to work.
Pay starts when you enter employment, and no later than 30 days after you enter the US under the petition.

#### Forms and fees

If you think you are not being paid correctly, tell any local Wage and Hour Division office, in writing or by phone. Form WH-4 is optional.
File within 12 months of the latest violation. There is no fee.

#### What to watch for

- You may never pay or reimburse the ACWIA training fee or the $500 fraud fee. Your employer also cannot take back its LCA and petition attorney fees through deductions from your pay.
- Pay deductions are allowed only if the law requires them, or you agreed in writing for your own benefit.
- A penalty for leaving early is not allowed. A genuine liquidated-damages clause is allowed only where state law permits.
- Your employer may not retaliate against you for raising a concern or helping an investigation.
- Employers keep a public access file with your LCA and wage documentation.
