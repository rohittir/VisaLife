#### What it is

On-campus jobs are allowed while you study, within limits.
Off-campus work during your program generally needs Curricular Practical Training (CPT) from your DSO.

#### When

- On campus: up to 20 hours a week while school is in session, and full time during breaks.
- New students can start on-campus work no more than 30 days before classes begin.
- CPT: only after your DSO authorizes it in SEVIS and gives you an endorsed I-20.

#### Forms and fees

No USCIS form or fee. Your DSO authorizes CPT.

#### What to watch for

- CPT must be part of your curriculum and directly related to your major.
- Twelve months or more of full-time CPT ends your eligibility for post-completion OPT.
- Never start a job before the authorization is in place.
