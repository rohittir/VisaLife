#### What it is

You paid Social Security and Medicare tax on your US wages.
There is no social security agreement between the US and India. So there is no certificate of coverage, and you cannot add US and Indian work together to qualify for a benefit.

#### When

Count your credits before you leave. You earn up to 4 credits a year. In 2026 one credit takes $1,890 of covered earnings.
A worker with 40 credits, about 10 years of work, can generally keep receiving a US retirement benefit while living in India.

#### Forms and fees

- Benefits abroad: without 40 credits, payments stop after six full calendar months outside the US, unless another exception applies. Family members and survivors must also have lived in the US for five years in that relationship.
- US tax on benefits: 85% of the benefit is taxed at a flat 30%. The India treaty does not reduce it. That is 25.5% of the payment. SSA sends Form SSA-1042S.
- Refunds: the IRS describes refunds of Social Security and Medicare tax only for tax taken out in error. Ask your employer first. F-1 students then use Form 843 with Form 8316.

#### What to watch for

- H-1B workers owe the tax from the first day. F-1 students are exempt on work tied to their visa until they become resident aliens for tax purposes.
- Medicare usually does not pay for care outside the US.
- Talk to a CPA about the tax on a benefit paid in India.
