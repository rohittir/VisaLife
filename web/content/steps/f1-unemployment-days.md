#### What it is

Post-completion OPT allows 90 days of unemployment in total.
With a STEM OPT extension, the total across both periods is 150 days.

#### When

Days count during your OPT whenever no qualifying job is reported.

#### Forms and fees

None.

#### What to watch for

- A job counts only if it is at least 20 hours a week and directly related to your major.
- Going over the limit ends your F-1 status, and SEVP can terminate your record.
- Keep your own count and enter it in Me so VisaLife can warn you.
