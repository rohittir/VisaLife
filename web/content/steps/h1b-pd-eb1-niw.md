#### What it is

Some people qualify for a green card without PERM.

- EB-1A is for extraordinary ability. You can file it yourself.
- EB-1B is for outstanding professors and researchers. An employer files it.
- EB-1C is for multinational managers and executives. An employer files it.
- The EB-2 National Interest Waiver (NIW) waives the job offer and PERM. You can file it yourself. USCIS uses a three-part test: your work has substantial merit and national importance, you are well positioned to advance it, and waiving the job offer benefits the US.

#### When

Any time you believe you meet the standard. You do not wait for PERM.

#### Forms and fees

Form I-140: $715 on paper, plus an asylum program fee listed on G-1055.
Premium processing costs $2,965. It takes 15 business days for EB-1A and EB-1B, and 45 business days for EB-1C and NIW.

#### What to watch for

- Each route has its own priority date. With no PERM, it is the date USCIS properly receives the I-140.
- You can hold more than one I-140. The earliest approved priority date is the one you use (8 CFR 204.5(e)).
- Each route sits on its own line of the bulletin. Check the India column for each.
- The evidence bar is high. Choosing a route is a strategic decision. Talk to an immigration attorney.
