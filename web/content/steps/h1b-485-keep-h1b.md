#### What it is

Filing an I-485 does not let you work by itself.
You have two ways to work while it is pending: stay in valid H-1B status, or use your EAD.
USCIS says that if you follow your H-1B terms, you need no EAD, and you may still apply for one if you prefer.

#### When

Each time your H-1B or your EAD comes up for renewal, you choose again.
The H-1B extension rules for AC21 continue until a final decision on your I-485.

#### Forms and fees

Extending H-1B: your employer files Form I-129. Premium processing costs $2,965.
Renewing the EAD: Form I-765. See the work permit step.

#### What to watch for

- A pending I-485 gives you no lawful status. It is a period of authorized stay, so you do not build up unlawful presence.
- If you work only on an EAD based on the I-485 and the I-485 is denied, the EAD may be terminated. You would have no H-1B to fall back on.
- Keeping a valid H-1B keeps the travel route on a visa stamp open.
- An EAD lasts at most 18 months now. Plan for renewals.
- Which is better for you is a strategic decision. Talk to an immigration attorney.
