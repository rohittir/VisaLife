#### What it is

When an H-1B job ends, the worker and their H-4 family keep their status for up to 60 days.
If the I-94 ends sooner, the grace period ends then.
This grace period is allowed once per authorized validity period.

#### When

The clock starts when the H-1B job ends.
Ask the employer to confirm the last day of employment in writing.

#### Forms and fees

Within the grace period, the options include:

- a new employer files an H-1B petition for your spouse,
- a change of status for your spouse or the family,
- an adjustment of status application, if eligible,
- a compelling-circumstances EAD, in some cases,
- leaving the US.

#### What to watch for

- An archived USCIS page says an H-4 spouse's EAD stays valid during the grace period. Current USCIS pages do not repeat this, so confirm before you rely on it.
- If nothing is filed in time, the family generally must leave.
- Changing status after a layoff is a strategic choice. Talk to an immigration attorney.
