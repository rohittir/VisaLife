#### What it is

Your US retirement money, your US Social Security and your Indian EPF each have their own rules once you live in India.

#### When

Before you return.
Decide what to do with your 401(k) before you become resident.

#### Forms and fees

- US 401(k) and IRA: a resident can choose to have the account's income taxed in India when it is taxed on withdrawal. The choice is Form 40 under Rule 74, made by the due date of your return. It covers all your accounts of that kind and cannot be withdrawn. Form 40 lists the US as a country.
- US Social Security: the India-US treaty lets the paying state, the US, tax these benefits. The US withholds 30% on 85% of the payment. There is no US-India social security agreement. See the Social Security step.
- EPF withdrawal: EPFO deducts 10% tax when the balance paid is Rs 50,000 or more and the 5-year rule is not met. A balance is not taxed if you had 5 or more years of continuous service, or if it moves to another recognised fund.
- RSUs and ESOPs: India taxes shares a current or former employer gives you free or at a concession as part of your pay. For options the value is the market value on the exercise date less what you paid.

#### What to watch for

- The pages we read do not say how India treats a US 401(k) lump sum, an RSU vest or an ESPP purchase. That includes a vest after you leave the US and before you arrive. Ask a CA.
- The Form 40 choice lapses if you later become non-resident.
- We could not read EPFO's own rules for inactive accounts after you move abroad. Ask EPFO or your former employer.
- Talk to a CA about the order of US cash-out tax, Indian tax and credit on the same money.
