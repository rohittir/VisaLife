#### What it is

With an I-485 pending, you can apply for a work permit (EAD) and a travel document (advance parole).
The EAD is category (c)(9). It lets you work for any employer.
Both can arrive on one "combo card".

#### When

File them with your I-485, or later with a copy of the I-485 receipt notice.
For a renewal, USCIS recommends filing up to 180 days before the card expires.

#### Forms and fees

- Form I-765, category (c)(9): $260 if you paid the I-485 fee on a form filed on or after April 1, 2024.
- Form I-131 for advance parole: $630 on paper, $580 online.

#### What to watch for

- The card is now valid for at most 18 months. That applies to requests pending or filed on or after Dec. 5, 2025. Before then it could be up to five years.
- Renewals filed on or after Oct. 30, 2025 get no automatic extension. Your permission to work ends the day after the card's end date.
- The no-automatic-extension change came from a DHS interim final rule. Check USCIS for any later change.
- Advance parole does not guarantee you will be let back in. CBP decides at the airport.
