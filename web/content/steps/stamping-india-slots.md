#### What it is

Visa interview slots in India are scarce.
Since December 2025, when the online-presence review began, consulates in India have moved many H-1B and H-4 interviews months later.
Applicants have reported long stretches with no regular slots at any of the five posts.
F-1 students book in the same system.

#### When

Start before you book any trip that needs a new stamp.
State's wait-time page, updated Sept. 17, 2026, predicts H and L interview waits of 1 month in Kolkata, 1.5 months in Chennai and Mumbai, 3 months in New Delhi and 5.5 months in Hyderabad.
State says these numbers are not a guarantee, and many applicants see no slots at all.

#### Forms and fees

- Where: if you are legally present in India, you can apply at New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad or Kolkata. Pick the post with the earliest date.
- No interview waiver: in India the waiver covers B-1/B-2 renewals and diplomatic visas only. H-1B, H-4 and F-1 applicants interview in person.
- Two appointments: everyone needs a biometrics appointment at a Visa Application Center and an interview at the consulate.
- Before you can book: a submitted DS-160 and a paid visa fee. The fee receipt can take up to one business day to become active.
- Reschedule once: India allows one reschedule. A second reschedule or a missed appointment means paying a new fee.
- Cancel or reschedule at least two business days before the appointment. After that you cannot.
- Expedite: you can ask only after you book the first available regular date. You need proof of an urgent, unforeseen need, and you get two requests. Weddings and graduations do not qualify.

#### What to watch for

- Slots are first come, first served. They open at unpredictable times, often a few at a time, and go within minutes.
- "No slots available" often means someone booked them before you looked.
- Have everything ready before you log in: a DS-160 for each person, an active fee receipt and correct passport details for everyone.
- H-1B and H-4 share the same calendar, so book the family in one session.
- Check the portal yourself, often. Group chats and alert apps reach you after others have seen the slot.
- Do not cancel a booked date to chase a better one, or while an expedite request is pending.
- VisaLife does not recommend paid slot services or agents. An agent's placeholder DS-160 loses you the appointment.
- On H-1B, a valid stamp from your previous employer still works with your new I-797. You may not need a new stamp at all.
- Do not leave the US needing a stamp until you have an interview date. Leave room for administrative processing after the interview.
- India's visa support desk is support-india@usvisascheduling.com.
- Talk to your employer's immigration lawyer, or your DSO on F-1, before you book travel.
