#### What it is

On STEM OPT, you complete self-evaluations on your I-983 training plan.
You and your employer both sign each one.

#### When

- First evaluation: 12 months after your STEM OPT start date.
- Final evaluation: at the end of the 24 months, or when you leave the job if that is sooner.
- Your DSO must receive each one within 10 days after the period ends.

#### Forms and fees

The evaluation section of Form I-983. No fee.

#### What to watch for

- Missing the final evaluation breaks the I-983 terms and can put your status at risk.
- A material change to your training plan needs a modified I-983, sent to your DSO as soon as possible.
- Examples of material changes: a pay cut not tied to fewer hours, or a big drop in weekly hours.
