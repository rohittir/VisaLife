#### What it is

Before the grace period ends, you need a next step that keeps you in status, or you leave the US.

#### When

A change of status must be filed while your status is still valid.

#### Forms and fees

- New program: your school transfers your SEVIS record. Classes must start within five months of your OPT end date or transfer-out date, whichever is earlier.
- Change of status, for example to H-4 if your spouse holds H-1B: Form I-539, $470 on paper or $420 online.
- Leaving the US: no form.

#### What to watch for

- Choosing a route after OPT is a strategic decision. Talk to an immigration attorney if you are unsure.
- Staying past the grace period without another status is a status violation.
