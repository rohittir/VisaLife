#### What it is

An H-4 EAD is a work permit for some H-4 spouses.
You are eligible if your H-1B spouse:

- is the main beneficiary of an approved Form I-140, or
- has H-1B time beyond six years under sections 106(a) and (b) of AC21.

You must be in H-4 status, and your spouse must be in H-1B status.

#### When

You can apply as soon as your spouse meets one of these conditions.

#### Forms and fees

Form I-765, category (c)(26). The next step covers how to file.

#### What to watch for

- Only spouses qualify. H-4 children cannot get this EAD.
- DHS has listed a plan to end H-4 EADs on its regulatory agenda (RIN 1615-AD14).
- As of this review, no proposed rule has been published and eligibility is unchanged. VisaLife could not load the agenda entry itself, so confirm its status there.
