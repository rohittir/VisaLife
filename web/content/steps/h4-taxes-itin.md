#### What it is

An ITIN is a tax ID number from the IRS.
You need one if you have a federal tax purpose and are not eligible for a Social Security number.
Being listed as a spouse on your partner's tax return counts as a federal tax purpose.

#### When

Apply with your federal tax return, by the return's due date.

#### Forms and fees

- Form W-7, sent in the same package as your tax return.
- You can apply by mail or in person.

#### What to watch for

- An ITIN does not give you permission to work.
- It does not change your immigration status.
