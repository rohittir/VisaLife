#### What it is

Every six months, you confirm your SEVIS details with your DSO, even if nothing changed.
You confirm your name, address, employer and employment status.

#### When

- Every six months from your STEM OPT start date.
- Report a change of name, address or employer, or the loss of a job, within 10 days.

#### Forms and fees

No form or fee.
SEVP Portal users get an email reminder 30 days before a validation is due.

#### What to watch for

- STEM OPT work must be at least 20 hours a week.
- Your unemployment limit is 150 days in total across OPT and STEM OPT.
