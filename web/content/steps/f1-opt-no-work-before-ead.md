#### What it is

You can start OPT work only on or after the start date on your EAD card.
Your authorization begins on the start date you asked for or the approval date, whichever is later.

#### When

From the day you file until the start date on the card.

#### Forms and fees

None. Keep your I-797 receipt notice and follow the case in your USCIS online account.

#### What to watch for

- You do not need a job offer to apply. You are expected to be employed once OPT starts.
- Once OPT starts, days without a reported job count as unemployment.
- Travel while the application is pending is risky. USCIS mails requests for evidence to your US address, and you need the EAD card in hand to re-enter once it is approved.
