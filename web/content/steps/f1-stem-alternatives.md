#### What it is

If you are not selected, other routes exist.
Each one has its own rules.

#### When

Your options narrow once STEM OPT and the grace period end, so look at them early.

#### Forms and fees

- A cap-exempt employer, such as a university or a related nonprofit, can file an H-1B petition without the lottery.
- A new degree program keeps you in F-1 status through a SEVIS transfer.
- A change of status, for example to H-4 if your spouse holds H-1B, uses Form I-539.

#### What to watch for

- Choosing between these routes is a strategic decision. Talk to an immigration attorney.
- For a SEVIS transfer, classes at the new school must start within five months of your OPT end date.
