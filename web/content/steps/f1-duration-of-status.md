#### What it is

F-1 students are admitted for "duration of status" (D/S).
Your I-94 shows D/S instead of a fixed date.
You stay in status while you study full time and during authorized practical training.

#### When

Today, D/S still applies.
DHS published a final rule on July 17, 2026 that would replace D/S with fixed I-94 dates.
A federal court postponed that rule on Sept. 14, 2026, one day before it was due to start.
USCIS says it is following the previous rules until that changes.

#### Forms and fees

Under D/S, you file nothing to extend your stay while you keep studying.
Under the paused rule, students needing more time would file Form I-539 with USCIS.

#### What to watch for

- Under the paused rule, admission would be capped at four years, and the grace period after your program would drop from 60 days to 30.
- DHS says it will put the rule in place if the court order is lifted, so this can change quickly.
- Check that your I-94 shows "D/S" after each entry.
